[ Yarnie ]

Scientific Name:Filamenta orbiculata

NAME OF YOUR CREATURE:Yarnie

Discovered by / at:

When a famous botanist Sara explored the tropical forest in Madagascar in 1958, several Yarnie climbed up from the soil

Family:

It’s a strange creature that not any creature seems to have a relation with Yarnie.

Habitat:

Everywhere you can imagine.

Appearance:

It has a round shape, and can rotate in the ground. It’s usually pink, but will change its color according to the environment. The Susuwatari from the film Spirited Away is inspired from it.

Key Characteristics:

  • Rotates all over the ground
  • Changes its color as a kind of expression or mood
  • Loves to leave its wool-like things behind
  • Is afraid of people so it will tremble

What we know so far about Yarnie? It’s a very strange creature because it seems that it is able to change its appearance like a combination of Mollusk and Chameleon. Also it is lowkey like a mimosa, becuse it is afraid of people.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature By moving the mouse, we can guide Yarnie to go to what we want them to, though unwillingly. It is so naughty that it likes to keep its traces by leaving part of its body behind. So scientists have to clean it up for it all the time.
